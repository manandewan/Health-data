# Clinical & Epidemiological Investigation: Occupational Health Cohort (N = 286)

A first-principles literary medical research monograph and interactive diagnostic intelligence portal analyzing **286 de-identified workplace screening profiles** across cardiometabolic, hematology, hepatology, and renal organ systems.

---

## 🏛️ Executive Clinical Summary

| Clinical Dimension | Cohort Empirical Finding | First-Principles Physiological Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Demographics** | **Median Age: 26.0 Yrs** (69.6% &lt;30) | Young active workforce with shift-work dietary & physical stressors |
| **Female Anemia** | **80.5% (33 of 41 Women)** | Severe microcytic hypochromic iron deficiency (MCV down to 58 fL) |
| **Suboptimal HDL** | **42.3% (120 of 284)** | Depressed reverse-cholesterol clearing capacity (&lt;40 mg/dL) |
| **Hypertriglyceridemia** | **40.1% (114 of 284)** | Excessive circulating fuel packets (&gt;150 mg/dL, max 922 mg/dL) |
| **Hepatic Transaminases** | **25.3% (72 of 285)** | Elevated ALT/SGPT (&gt;49 U/L, max 402 U/L), signaling silent MASLD |
| **Systemic Inflammation** | **28.1% (79 of 281)** | Elevated ESR (&gt;12 mm/1st hr, max 94 mm/1st hr) |
| **Renal Parenchyma** | **Mean eGFR = 117.2 mL/min** | Intact baseline filtration reserves (98.6% normal creatinine) |

## 📖 Report Architecture

The digital research monograph is structured into comprehensive first-principles chapters:

1. **Chapter 1: The Workforce & Population Anatomy** — Demographic cross-tabulations and baseline biological reserves.
2. **Chapter 2: The Cardiometabolic Engine** — The physiology of Triglycerides, HDL, LDL, and HbA1c; decoding the South Asian *Atherogenic Dyslipidemia* phenomenon in young adults.
3. **Chapter 3: The Female Anemia Crisis** — Erythropoiesis, oxygen transport, microcytic sizing (MCV/MCH), and Mentzer Index differential diagnosis.
4. **Chapter 4: The Hepatic Refinery** — Why hepatocytes leak transaminases (ALT/AST) under lipid overload; silent non-alcoholic fatty liver (MASLD) detection.
5. **Chapter 5: Renal Filtration & Endocrine Thermostat** — Creatinine clearance, eGFR, purine crystal accumulation (uric acid), and pituitary TSH feedback.
6. **Chapter 6: Multi-Tier Clinical Action Protocol** — Phased, evidence-based workplace interventions (supervised oral iron, dietary canteen reform, hydration, screening).
7. **Chapter 7: Cohort Explorer & Diagnostic Dossiers** — Searchable, filterable interactive database of all 286 de-identified individuals with individual clinical dossiers.
